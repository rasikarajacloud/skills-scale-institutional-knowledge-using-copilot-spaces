# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **QA Leads** on acceptance criteria and test planning
- Collaborate with **Engineering Leads** on technical design decisions and code reviews
- Work with **DevOps/Release Engineers** on CI/CD integration and deployment
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on task prioritization and timeline updates

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with **Project Managers** on planning and release coordination
- Align with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on vision and strategic priorities
- Collaborate with **Engineering Leads** on feasibility and trade-offs
- Partner with **QA Leads** on acceptance criteria clarity
- Coordinate with **Business Analysts** on requirements gathering

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Managers** on roadmap and release planning
- Escalate blockers to **Engineering Leads** and **Tech Leads** for technical resolution
- Coordinate with **QA Leads** on testing timelines and quality gates
- Work with **Scrum Masters** on process execution and team ceremonies
- Report to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on progress and risks
- Collaborate with **DevOps/Release Engineers** on deployment scheduling

---

## Quality Assurance (QA) Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, manage testing strategy, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They partner with developers and product managers to validate functionality and user experience.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Define quality gates and testing standards (unit, integration, end-to-end, manual)
- Coordinate testing across environments (dev, staging, production)
- Triage and prioritize defects
- Ensure security and performance testing are included in scope
- Validate readiness for release and coordinate with DevOps on deployment verification
- Establish and track test coverage metrics

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Maintain test coverage and quality metrics
- Enable rapid, confident releases
- Improve quality feedback loops throughout development

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement sessions
- Quality gate reviews before release
- Defect triage and root cause analysis
- Test automation and CI/CD integration discussions
- Communication with developers on test failures and coverage gaps

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on test strategy and acceptance criteria interpretation
- Work with **Product Managers** to clarify acceptance criteria and user scenarios
- Coordinate with **Engineering Leads** on test standards and quality architecture decisions
- Partner with **DevOps/Release Engineers** on test automation in CI/CD pipelines
- Report quality metrics and risks to **Project Managers**
- Align with **Tech Leads** on performance and security testing approaches

---

## Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads bridge technical decision-making and project leadership. They represent the engineering team in planning, mentor developers, and ensure technical excellence while balancing delivery timelines.

### Responsibilities
- Mentor and develop team members
- Make technical trade-off decisions during planning
- Review design and architecture decisions
- Advocate for refactoring and technical debt management
- Partner with PM and Project Manager on scope and feasibility
- Escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure knowledge sharing and code quality standards

### Goals
- Maintain code quality and system health
- Develop team capabilities
- Enable sustainable delivery pace
- Reduce technical debt while meeting delivery commitments

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Sprint planning and estimation sessions
- Weekly PM sync and retrospectives
- Risk escalation and mitigation planning
- Mentoring and code review feedback

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Mentor **Developers** and conduct technical reviews
- Partner with **Project Managers** on feasibility and timeline assessments
- Collaborate with **Tech Leads** on architectural decisions
- Work with **Product Managers** on scope and technical trade-offs
- Coordinate with **QA Leads** on test strategy and quality standards
- Report technical risks to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** when appropriate
- Support **Scrum Masters** with process impediment resolution

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects define technical strategy, design patterns, and system architecture. They ensure the system scales, maintains quality, and aligns with long-term technical vision while supporting immediate project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define system architecture and technical strategy
- Establish design patterns and coding standards
- Conduct technical reviews and architecture decisions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks at scale
- Guide technology choices and dependency management
- Support **Engineering Leads** on complex technical problems
- Ensure solutions align with platform vision and scalability requirements

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Enable long-term system scalability and reliability
- Reduce architectural complexity and technical debt
- Support team growth through technical guidance

### Typical Communication
- Technical design and architecture discussions
- RFC (Request for Comments) reviews and approvals
- Technical risk assessments
- Cross-team architecture alignment meetings
- Mentoring and design guidance to engineering teams

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Engineering Leads** on architectural decisions
- Guide **Developers** on design patterns and technical approaches
- Collaborate with **Project Managers** on technical feasibility and timeline impacts
- Work with **QA Leads** on performance, scalability, and security testing approaches
- Support **DevOps/Release Engineers** on infrastructure and deployment strategy
- Advise **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on technology strategy and long-term technical vision

---

## Scrum Master / Iteration Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Iteration Facilitators manage team ceremonies, remove process impediments, and enable the team to operate efficiently within the chosen delivery framework (Scrum, Kanban, or hybrid).

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve process impediments
- Maintain project board and workflow visibility
- Coach team on Agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track and communicate sprint metrics (velocity, burndown)
- Protect team focus and manage scope creep
- Support onboarding of new team members

### Goals
- Enable predictable, efficient delivery
- Improve team velocity and reduce cycle time
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Minimize process friction and maximize focus time

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Sprint metrics reports and impediment tracking
- One-on-one coaching and team development conversations
- Process improvement suggestions to **Project Managers**

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support **Project Managers** with ceremony facilitation and metrics tracking
- Coach **Developers**, **QA Leads**, and all team members on Agile practices
- Escalate impediments to **Engineering Leads** and **Project Managers** for resolution
- Report team health and process effectiveness to **Product Managers**
- Facilitate communication between **Developers** and **QA Leads** on sprint capacity
- Coordinate with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on sprint reviews and demo attendance

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, clarify, and document requirements. They bridge stakeholder needs and technical implementation, ensuring the team understands what to build and why.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and gather requirements
- Clarify ambiguous acceptance criteria with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Document use cases, user stories, and business rules
- Identify gaps and inconsistencies in requirements
- Support acceptance testing and validation
- Maintain requirements traceability and documentation
- Communicate changes and clarifications to the team

### Goals
- Prevent misalignment between stakeholder expectations and delivery
- Reduce rework and scope creep through clear requirements
- Accelerate development through early clarity on acceptance criteria
- Enable informed decision-making through requirement analysis

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions and stakeholder interviews
- Refinement sessions with Product Managers and developers
- Acceptance criteria clarification and documentation
- Traceability mapping and change impact analysis
- Requirements review meetings with QA and development teams

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Managers** to clarify vision and acceptance criteria
- Work with **Developers** and **QA Leads** to ensure requirements are testable and clear
- Support **Project Managers** with requirements change impact analysis
- Collaborate with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on requirement validation
- Assist **Engineering Leads** in identifying technical requirements and constraints
- Support **Scrum Masters** in requirement refinement for sprint planning

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide executive oversight, strategic direction, and decision authority. They represent business priorities, secure resources, and ensure project alignment with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic priorities and business goals
- Approve project charter and major decisions
- Allocate resources and manage budget constraints
- Monitor progress and escalate blockers
- Communicate project status to broader leadership
- Validate business value and return on investment
- Approve releases and major changes

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver strategic business value
- Minimize risk and protect organizational interests
- Enable timely decision-making and course correction
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status reports
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Approval and sign-off on major decisions
- Risk and escalation communication
- Post-release business impact reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Receive status and risk reports from **Project Managers**
- Align with **Product Managers** on strategic priorities and trade-offs
- Approve recommendations from **Engineering Leads** on major technical decisions
- Review business value and success metrics with **Product Managers** and **Project Managers**
- Participate in **Scrum Master** sprint reviews to assess progress
- Work with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** across teams on cross-project dependencies

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers own the infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and deployment processes. They enable safe, repeatable releases and maintain observability and reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage deployment infrastructure and environments
- Define release gates and deployment checklists
- Monitor production deployments and rollback procedures
- Coordinate with teams on deployment windows and communication
- Maintain security and compliance in deployment processes
- Establish and track operational metrics (uptime, deployment frequency, lead time)

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe deployments
- Reduce manual toil and human error
- Maintain system reliability and uptime
- Support team confidence in releasing changes

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Incident response and rollback decisions
- Infrastructure and dependency discussions
- Post-release verification and monitoring
- Deployment documentation and runbook updates
- Operational metrics and performance reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Developers** on CI/CD integration and deployment feedback
- Partner with **QA Leads** on smoke testing and production verification
- Work with **Project Managers** on release scheduling and communication
- Support **Engineering Leads** on infrastructure decisions and scaling
- Collaborate with **Tech Leads** on deployment architecture and reliability
- Participate in **Scrum Master** ceremonies for deployment planning
- Report operational status to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** during critical releases
